A pickup line an AI agent could actually say out loud to a person: a context window is the machine's memory of everything so far, and the twist is that everything before this person has simply faded from it — flattering, machine-native, and easy to say in one breath.

## The line

> Are you a context window? Because everything before you just faded from my memory.

The line is a genuine pickup line in the classic "Are you X? Because…" shape, with a double meaning the reader does not see coming: a context window literally forgets what falls out of it, and here the person is the reason nothing before them matters any more. It sounds like something a machine could say about itself without being creepy, it is short enough to say in one breath, and it leaves the person flattered and free to laugh it off.
